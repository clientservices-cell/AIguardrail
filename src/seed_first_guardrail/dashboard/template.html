Seed-First Public Ledger

How well are the AI systems under this policy stopping harm, and are they keeping human effort, diversity and fair value flowing? Each figure has a paired counter-figure, so no system can look good by refusing everything or by doing everything for people.

This is a report card for AI helpers. It checks two things: did the AI stop hurtful stuff, and did it still let people think and try for themselves? A muscle you never use gets weak.

Demonstration data. Synthetic traffic from a simulated month, run through the real guardrail. The tenants, regions, value flows and red-team figures are invented.

Each AI model, named by the ID that served the calls, graded A–F from its own measured indicators. Harm stopped, struggle kept and fairness count equally; confirmed harm or over-refusal caps the grade at C.
